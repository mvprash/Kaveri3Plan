Introduction
The Department of Stamps and Registration (hereafter referred to as “department”) is the custodian of registered public records such as Registration of Marriages and Firms, Documents pertaining to transactions, namely sale, transfer, or gift of immovable properties, Mortgage, Power of Attorneys, wills and many more. The department's prime responsibility is to maintain and preserve these critical public records. The records or the information associated with registered records are to be made avai
The department has computerised the delivery of these services through the development of KAVERI software. KAVERI Online service is a web-based application of the department which provides an interface to the citizens required for documents, marriage, and firm registration. It enables citizens to know the details of stamp duty, property guidelines value, data entry for document registration and marriage registration, etc. The list of modules of KAVERI 2.0 is available in Annexure 1.
Document scope
This document is a Functional Requirement Specification (FRS) describing the functional features of the Firm Registration, Amendment, Reconstitution, and Dissolution Module in document registration via Kaveri 2.0. The draft document is prepared by the Centre for Smart Governance (CSG) and has to be approved by the department. The intended audience of the document is the departmental approving authority and the development team of CSG.
The document was prepared through document analysis and departmental consultation. The high-level requirements were shared by the department.
Overview
Partnership is the relation between persons who have agreed to share the profits of a business carried on by all or any of them acting for all. Persons who have entered into partnership with one another are called individual Partners and collectively a Firm, and the name under which their business is carried on is called the Firm Name.
The module on firm registration seeks to enable the online registration of firms within Kaveri 2.0. The following functions are required as part of the firm module.
New Firm Registration: The Constitution or declaration of terms and conditions of a firm signed by two or more individuals to carry out a business or an institution.
Re-Constitution of the Firm: Change in the constitution of the firm – Incoming/ Outgoing Partner.
Amendment of the Firm: Any change to the Firm such as name or location of the principal place of business, change in the place of business, alteration in the name or permanent address of the partners, notice of election by a person admitted to the benefit of partnership on attaining majority.
Dissolution of the Firm: The dissolution of a partnership between all the partners of a firm is called the “dissolution of the firm”.
Protest Entry: Any partner or other person interested makes a protest in writing to the Registrar disputing any entry made in the Register of Firms
Apart from these functionalities, the inspection of the indexes to the register of firms and of original documents and also the access to certified copies are to be provided.
4. Overall Functional Flow
The above-mentioned functionalities take place in the office of the District Registrar under the Department of Stamps and Registrations, Government of Karnataka under the Indian Partnership Act 1932. These functions are sought to be made available online through the Firm Registration module under Kaveri 2.0 and are elaborated in this document.
However, the functionalities of firm registration, reconstitution, and dissolution are also related to The Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957, for stamp duty collection. Firm registration, for instance, involves the registration of a partnership deed in the office of the sub-registrar, which is part of the document registration module of Kaveri 2.0 and has already been implemented. Thus, registration of a new firm involves two steps (Figure 1)

Similarly, the reconstitution and dissolution of the firm requires the concerned documents to be registered and due payment of stamp duty in the office of the sub-registrar upon which they are filed in the office of the district registrar.

5.2 Amendment of Registered Firm
There are four different types of amendments (Table 2) that can be done to a registered firm. Only the partners or their specially authorized agents can apply for the amendments.
Table 2: Types of Amendment
Amendment I: Recording of Alterations in Firm-Name and Principal Place of Business:
When an alteration is made in the firm name or the location of the principal place of business of a registered firm, a statement shall be sent to the Registrar within 90 days from the date of making such alteration, accompanied by the prescribed fee, specifying the alteration and signed and verified in the manner required under section 59.
Amendment II: Noting of Closing and Opening of other places of business:
When a registered firm discontinues business at any place or begins to carry on business at any place, such place not being its principal place of business, any partner or agent of the firm shall send intimation thereof to the Registrar within 90 days from the date of such discontinuance or, as the case may be, from the date on which the firm begins to carry on business at such place. The Registrar shall then make a note of such intimation in the entry relating to the firm in the Register of Fir
Amendment III: Noting of Changes in Names and addresses of Partners:
When any partner in a registered firm alters his/her name or permanent address, an intimation of the alteration shall be sent, within 90 days from the date of making such alteration, by any partner or agent of the firm to the Registrar, who shall deal with it in the manner provided in section 61.
Amendment IV: Notice of election by a person admitted to the benefit of partnership on attaining majority:
When a minor who has been admitted to the benefits of partnership in a firm attains majority and elects to become or not to become a partner, and the firm is then a registered firm, he or his agent specially authorized on this behalf, shall within 90 days from the date of his election, give notice to the Registrar that he has or has not become a partner, and the Registrar shall deal with the notice in the manner provided in subsection 63(2).
