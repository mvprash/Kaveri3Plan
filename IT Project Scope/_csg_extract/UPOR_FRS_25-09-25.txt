P39|Kaveri 2.0
P40|The Department of Stamps and Registration (hereinafter referred to as the “Department”) is entrusted with the custody, maintenance, and preservation of registered public records, including but not limited to the registration of marriages and firms, documents evidencing transactions such as sale, transfer, or gift of immovable properties, mortgages, powers of attorney, wills, and other instruments of legal or commercial significance. The Department, in addition to its custodial functions, is responsible for the levy and collection of stamp duty on behalf of the Government for transactions and instruments as prescribed under law. These services are administered through 259 Sub-Registrar Offices (SROs), operating under the supervision of 35 District Registrar Offices across the State. The list of modules of KAVERI 2.0 is available in Annexure 1.
P41|Document scope
P42|This document is a Functional Requirement Specification (FRS) describing the functional features and integration protocol for UPOR properties in Kaveri 2.0. The document is prepared by the Centre for Smart Governance (CSG) and has been submitted for department approval. The intended audience of the document is the departmental approving authority and the development team.
P43|Overview of the function
P45|Figure: workflow for application submission
P46|Currently in Kaveri 2.0, citizen can proceed with property search and register the document which involves the agricultural properties which lies within the BHOOMI database. Upon UPOR integration, citizen now proceed with the registration of the document which involves the agricultural properties falling under UPOR category.
P48|Functional Requirements for UPOR
P49|4.1 Process flow
P54|4.1.1 Article Selection
P55|1. User will select nature of documents, stamp article and sub-article.
P56|2. User will be allowed to select same articles that are mapped to agricultural properties (BHOOMI). Refer to annexure 2 for list of articles.
P57|4.1.2 Property Search
P58|3. User will be provided to select the type of agricultural properties as listed below:
P59|BHOOMI
P60|UPOR
P62|4. Once user select UPOR option, user will be asked to enter following details:
P63|Bhoomi village name
P64|Road name
P65|UPOR number
P67|5. Once the property is fetched, the user will be allowed to select the owner's name and extent.
P69|6. The user can select the full extent for a complete transaction. However, in case of part transaction the user should select part extent.
P71|7. There will be no requirement for an 11E sketch for part transactions. Instead, the user will be provided with an option to enter the area to be transacted in sq. meters and this area should be considered for further calculations. Any area above 1 sq metre and less than the fetched property area will be transactable using the part extent option.
P73|8. The user should enter the boundary details, description of the schedule and proceed further.
P75|4.1.3 Party Details
P76|9. Imported parties: The executants for immovable properties will be fetched from the property registers (UPOR).
P78|10. The fetched user details will not be editable.
P80|11. The next process will be similar to the existing flow of document registration till market valuation.
P82|4.1.4 Market valuation and Fee calculations
P83|12. Market valuation will be based on the road name selected by the user during property selection. Further, the process will be similar to the document registration for agricultural properties (i.e Bhoomi).
P85|13. All calculations for agricultural properties happen in acre/gunta/fgunta as unit of measurement (UoM). However, since the UPOR area is in sqmt, it will be converted to acre/gunta/fgunta for market valuation purposes.
P87|14. Similar to the existing logic for market valuation, if the area is less than 5 gunta (after conversion from sqmt), non-agricultural rates will apply.
P89|15. The following rates will be applicable when agricultural land fetched from UPOR property records.
P90|If the agricultural land is 20 cents or less in area: The site rate of the respective area concerned shall be applicable.
P91|If the land measuring more than 20 cents and less than 40 cents in area: The price to be applied shall be 80% of the site price of the respective area concerned or the price of agricultural land, whichever is highest.
P92|If the land measuring above 40 cents: The price to be applied shall be 60% of the site price of the respective area concerned or the price of agricultural land, whichever is highest.
P94|16. User should select the annexures as per Agri properties annexures.
P96|17. The further application should follow typical registration flow.
P98|18. During fee calculation, two additional fees will be applicable:
P99|a. UPOR service fee: The amount will be fetched from the API provided by UPOR.
P100|b. Mutation fee:
P102|19. The UPOR service fee will have a separate HoA.
P104|20. The HoA for the mutation fee will be the same as that used for Bhoomi.
P106|21. Mutation XMLs will be sent once the registration is complete, as is the case for Bhoomi properties.
P107|Assumptions
P108|All the validation such GL, GR, PYKI and alienated land must be verified at UPOR. Kaveri will allow the registration of property that fetched from UPOR.
P109|There will not be multiple owner like mojini, instead a property can be owned by multiple owners jointly.
P110|Unit of measurement will always be sq. meter.
P111|The user can transact between 1 sq. meter till the area fetched UPOR.
P112|UPOR will only be available in 37 villages. See annexure for the list of such villages.
P113|J-slips will be sent for the registered documents involving UPOR properties.
