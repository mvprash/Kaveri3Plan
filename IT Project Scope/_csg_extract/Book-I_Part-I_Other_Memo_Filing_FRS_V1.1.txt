P54|Memo filing
P55|Document scope
P56|This document is a Functional Requirement Specification (FRS) describing the functional features of the Memo Filing for document registration. While Memo Filing is already available for documents that are registered on Kaveri 2.0, the current FRS describes the functionality when such memo filing must be done for document registrations that happen or have happened in Kaveri 1.0, Prior Kaveri 1 and Memos sent by SROs of other states.
P58|Overview of the function
P60|Figure :Memo Filing function after Document registration.
P62|The functionalities mentioned above occur in the sub registrar’s office under the Department of Stamps and Registrations, Government of Karnataka, under the Karnataka Registration Rules, 1965. This document elaborates on these functions and seeks to be available online through the Filing module under Kaveri 2.0.
P63|Documents must be registered in the jurisdictional SRO where the property is located. Memo filing is necessary if the properties are under different SROs' jurisdiction and this process facilitates internal communication between SROs for properties outside their jurisdiction.
P65|This document refers to the Memo filing module that handles the following cases,
P66|Kaveri 1 Memos – A memo generated in the Kaveri-1 system, and not filed yet, which is being filed in the present Kaveri 2 application.
P67|Pre Kaveri-Memos – The memos generated before the Kaveri-1 application and not filed yet, which is being filed in the present Kaveri 2 application.
P68|Other State to Kaveri 2— A memo shared by other states' SRO when a property from Karnataka is registered in other states.

P91|Process Description
P92|Once the registration is completed, registering SRO (Kaveri 1/ prior Kaveri/ other state) generates a hard copy of the memo (Form 25) manually.
P93|A hard copy memo shall be shared with the Kaveri 2 jurisdiction SRO for filing.
P94|Start process
P95|Kaveri 2 jurisdiction SRO receives the memo (hard copy) and initiates the filing process by clicking the Other Memo sub-tab under the Memo filing tab.
P96|SR should select where the document is registered using the radio buttons (in Karnataka or Other States).
P97|If the document is registered in Karnataka, then SR selects whether the document is registered before 2004 or after 2004 using the radio buttons.
P98|Based on the selection the SR should be able to key in the following details:

P197|SR receives the report after corrections from DEO to ‘Check and Register’.

P199|SR receives the report from FDA/SDA or self-verified application for check and register.
P200|Verify the report, and click on the ‘Check and register’.
P201|SR should allocate to DEO to print the certificate and upload the documents.

P203|DEO should print the certificate and take the SR’s signature on the certificate.
P204|Upload the Signed certificate, Memo copy and other documents for digital signature.

P212|Provide a note on EC with the registration number and SRO.
