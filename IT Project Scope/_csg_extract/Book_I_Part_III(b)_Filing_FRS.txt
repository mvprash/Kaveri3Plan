P55|Book-1 Part III(b): Land Acquisition statement
P56|Overview
P57|To urbanize, authorities such as BDA, MUDA, TUDA, KIADB, etc., will acquire revenue land under the Land Acquisition Act 1894. The Relevant authorities will send a copy of the statement to the jurisdictional registering officer, who will file it and make an entry in Book No-1, electronically for storing and preserving it.

P62|The below-mentioned functionalities take place in the office of the Sub Registrar under the Department of Stamps and Registration of Government of Karnataka under the Karnataka Registration Rules, 1965. Filing module functionalities of Kaveri 2.0 are elaborated in this document.

P74|Process Description
P75|The Relevant Authorities will send the Land acquisition order to the concerned jurisdictional SRO (manually) for filing.
P76|Filing process
P77|K2 jurisdictional SRO initiates the process by entering the Issuing authority, Date of Issue and notification number and allocates the task of inputting details from the Land acquisition statement to DEO.
P79|DEO - Data entry
P80|DEO inputs details according to the Land acquisition statement:
P81|Property details.
P82|Boundary details.
P83|Schedule Description.
P84|Party details.
P85|DEO repeats the above step until he adds all the properties mentioned in the land acquisition order and once he finishes then proceeds to the next step.
P86|DEO generates the report and sends it back to SR for approval.
P87|SR can either self-verify or allocate to the FDA for Verification.
P89|SR - FDA/ SDA allocation
P90|SR selects the FDA/SDA from the drop-down and clicks on the allocate button.
P91|FDA/SDA receives the report in the dashboard for verification.
P93|SR - Self Verification
P94|SR clicks on ‘Verify’ button to verify the report.
P95|SR adds remarks if any and sends it back to DEO for editing/correcting.
P96|If there are no correction SR approves for registration.
P98|DEO - Correction
P99|DEO views the remarks given by SR/FDA/SDA, updates them and sends the modified report to them.
P101|FDA/SDA - Verification
P102|FDA/SDA receives the report with corrections in the dashboard for verification from DEO.
P103|FDA/SDA verifies the report and sends it back to SR for approval.
P105|SR - Approval
P106|SR receives the report after modification in the dashboard for verification from DEO.
P107|SR receives the report for approval from the FDA/SDA.
P108|SR approves the report Summary and Check and Register.
P109|Once SR registers, DEO generate endorsement, prints it and will take a printout of endorsement.
P110|DEO takes the physical signature of the SR on the endorsement, uploads physically signed endorsement, annexures and sends them to SR for Digital sign.
P113|SR – Digital Sign
P114|SR signs on the hard copy of the Endorsement and had it over to DEO to scan and upload endorsement and Annexures.
P115|Once the SR receives the application in dashboard to digitally sign the document.
P116|SR adds digital sign on endorsement and all the pages uploaded with it.

P124|Input and Output Validations
P125|Input validations
P126|A minimum of 1 executant and 1 claimant are required, without which DEO cannot proceed further.
P127|At least 1 property detail should be entered to continue with the application.
P128|All text fields will have a maximum of 250-character limits.
P129|Description field is an alphanumeric field which should have 1000 characters limit including all the special characters.
P130|Without entering the mandatory fields application should not proceed further.
P131|Workflow validations
P132|SRO should be able to select and allocate to DEO.
P133|In case of DEO’s absence or FDA’s absence, the system should have the provision of a suggested in-charge.
P134|System provides a unique Filing number to the submitted application.
P135|All entries in each book shall be numbered in a consecutive series, which shall commence and terminate with the year, a fresh series being commenced at the beginning of each year.
