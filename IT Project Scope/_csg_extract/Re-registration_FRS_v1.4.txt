1 Introduction  
The Department of Stamps and Registration  (henceforth referred to as “department”)  is the 
custodian of registered public records such as Regist ration  of Marriages and Firms, Documents 
about transactions , namely sale, transfer or gift of immova ble properties, Mortgage, Power of 
Attorney , wills and many more.  Maintenance and preservation of these critical public records is the 
prime responsibility of the department. The records or the information associated with registered 
records are to be made available to the public when requested through defined procedures. The 
department is also responsible for collecting stamp duty on behalf of the Government for specified 
transactions. The services are provided through 25 9 Sub-Registrar Offices (SROs), unde r the 
supervision  of 35 District Registrar Offices, spread across the state.  
The department has computerised  the delivery of these services through the development of 
Kaveri software. The department's KAVERI Online service is a web -based application that provides 
an interface to Applicant s required for documents, marriage , and firm registration. It enables 
Applicant s to know the details of stamp duty, property guidelines value, data ent ry for document 
registration and marriage registration , etc. The list of modules of Kaveri 2.0 is available in Annexure 
1.

3 Overview of the function  
 
Fig: Re-registration function in Document registration . 
The re -registration function is part of the d ocument registration module. In the normal course of 
document registration, upon presentation of the document for registration, the document is 
registered , and the SR makes an entry in Book 1. However, a  Sub-Register can refuse to register a 
document upon its presentation1. In such cases, an entry is made in Book 2 , and the document is 
not registered. However, the applicant can appeal against such an order to the District Registrar  
(DR)2/Any competent court  within 30 days from the date of refusal . Upon the instructions of the 
DR/Court , the document is registered by the SR as if it were  presented for the first time , with the 
usual procedure of document registration3. Re -registration specifically concerns document 
registrations previously refused by SR but now  permitted by the DR4/Court .

Appeal to DR:  
The applicant collects the refusal endorsement from the SR, makes an appeal to the DR for 
verification of the d etails, and requests that the DR issue an order to re -register the document.  
i) DR views the document in Book 2.  
ii) Verifies the details and issues an order in favour of the applicant.  
iii) DR enters the following details  
(1) Year of Registration: Mandatory field (drop -down) starting with 1980 -81. 
(2) Appeal Date: Mandatory field (calendar).  
(3) DR Order Date: Mandatory field (calendar).  
iv) DR allocates to DEO to upload  the documents.  
(1) DR Order: Provision to upload DR order, A PDF of max size 5 MB.  
(2) Original Deed: Provision to upload the Original Deed copy, A PDF of max size 5 MB.  
v) DEO sends  back to DR for verification.  
vi) DR verifies the details and completes the process . 
vii) Applicant visits the SR office and re -presents the document once again.  
viii) Request SR to Re -register the document.  
Re-registration:  
i) The applicant visits the SRO with the DR order and other required documents.  
ii) SR opens the ‘ Book 2 ’ dashboard, locates the document in the list, and clicks on the  release 
button.  
iii) The system will prompt SR to verify and following details.  
(1) Year of Registration.  
(2) Appeal Date  
(3) DR Order Number.  
(4) DR Order Date.  
iv) The SR Keys in the presenter’s name and Remarks, and clicks on Re -register to proceed 
further.  
(1) Represented by: Mandatory field, a  text box with a  limit of 100 characters.  
(2) Remarks: Mandatory field, alphanumeric field with a limit of 1000 characters.  
v) After keying in the details, SR clicks the  ‘Save and Continue’ button, and the application will 
proceed to Step 7 of SR.  
vi) Then, the typical registration flow is  to be followed
