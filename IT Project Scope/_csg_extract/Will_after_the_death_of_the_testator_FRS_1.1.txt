P35|Introduction
P37|The Department of Stamps and Registration (henceforth referred to as “department”) is the custodian of registered public records such as Registration of Marriages and Firms, Documents about transactions, namely sale, transfer or gift of immovable properties, Mortgage, Power of Attorney, wills and many more. Maintenance and preservation of these critical public records is the prime responsibility of the department. The records or the information associated with registered records are to be made available to the public when requested through defined procedures. The department is also responsible for collecting stamp duty on behalf of the Government for specified transactions. The services are provided through 258 Sub-Registrar Offices (SROs), under the supervision of 35 District Registrar Offices, spread across the state.
P38|The department has computerised the delivery of these services through the development of Kaveri software. KAVERI Online service is a web-based application of the department that provides an interface to the citizens required for documents, marriage, and firm registration. It enables citizens to know the details of stamp duty, property guidelines value, data entry for document registration and marriage registration, etc. The list of modules of Kaveri 2.0 is available in Annexure 1.
P40|Document scope
P42|This document is a Functional Requirement Specification (FRS) describing the functional features of the Registration of wills after the death of the testator in Kaveri 2.0. The document is prepared by the Centre for Smart Governance (CSG) and is being submitted for the department’s approval. The intended audience of the document is the departmental approving authority and the development team.
P43|The document is prepared through document analysis and departmental consultation. The department shared the high-level requirements verbally. Following this, the CSG team analysed the requirement and prepared the FRS document.
P46|Overview of the function
P49|Fig: Will after the death of the testator features in the Document registration.
P50|A citizen has created a will but could not deposit it due to unforeseen circumstances, and passes away without registering the will, such a will can still be registered at the sub-registrar's office. The sub-registrar will electronically file a copy of the will in Book No. 3 and ensure its proper storage and preservation per regulatory requirements.
P51|The functionalities mentioned below take place in the office of the Sub-registrar under the Department of Stamps and Registrations, Government of Karnataka, under the Karnataka Registration Act, 1908. These functions are elaborated in this document and are sought to be available online through the document registration module under Kaveri 2.0.

P67|Process description
P68|Application Submission
P69|The applicant logs into the Kaveri 2.0 portal and selects the article for "Registration of Wills After the Death of the Testator."
P70|The system prompts the applicant to provide relevant details.
P71|The system should not allow user to choose consenting witness, private attendance and sec 88 exemption.
P72|The applicant enters key information, including the date of execution of the will, testator details, presenter details, and witness information.
P73|The system shows the fees to be paid by the citizen.
P74|The system then prompts the screen to upload the documents.
P75|The applicant uploads the will document, the death certificate of the testator, and proof of identity for both the presenter and witnesses.
P76|Once all information and documents are provided, the applicant applies.
P77|The system forwards the application to the SR for further scrutiny.
P78|Application Scrutiny
P79|The SR receives the application on their dashboard for verification.
P80|The SR has the option to either scrutinise the application personally or allocate it to an FDA/ SDA for initial verification.
P81|Application Scrutiny by FDA/SDA
P82|The FDA/SDA accesses the application via their dashboard.
P83|They verify the details and supporting documents provided in the application.
P84|Based on their assessment, the FDA/SDA either adds remarks or forwards the application to the SR for further scrutiny.
P85|Application Scrutiny by SR
P86|The SR reviews the application forwarded by the FDA/SDA.
P87|If corrections are needed, the SR adds remarks and sends the application back to the applicant for rectification.
P88|The system notifies the applicant about the required corrections.
P89|The applicant reviews the remarks, makes the necessary changes, and resubmits the application for verification.
P90|Once the application is verified and no corrections are required, the SR proceeds to the payment stage.
P91|Scheduling an Appointment
P92|The applicant makes the payment and schedules an appointment for the presentation of the application at the SRO.
P94|Application Presentation
P95|On the scheduled date, the applicant visits the SRO to present the application.
P96|The SR accepts the application for registration and assigns it to the DEO for biometric capture.
P97|The system initiates the SAKALA.
P98|The DEO captures the presenter’s biometrics and generates a summary, forwarding it back to the SR for approval.
P99|The SR reviews the summary received from the DEO and will hold the application for further investigation.
P100|The SR clicks on the ‘Keep Pending’ button to conduct the investigation, and the system prompts the reason “Keep pending for Deposition Statement of witnesses and Scribe” to select.
P101|Once the SR select the reason and continues, the system should move the application into the Minute Book tab and close the SAKALA.
P102|The SR conduct the investigation offline and uploads the investigation summary and releases the application.
P103|Registration of wills
P104|After the investigation, the SR uploads the recorded statements to release the application from the Minute book.
P105|The system releases the application from the Investigation of wills and routes it back to the SR for summary approval.
P106|If the SR is satisfied with the investigation, then approve the application by selecting the "Check and Register" button.
P107|Upon click on Check and Register button, the system displays the pop-up u with following checklist -
P108|That the will was executed by testator.
P109|That the testator id dead.
P110|That the person presenting the will is entitled to present the same.
P111|The SR selects the above checklist to register the will.
P112|Then typical registration flow will continue.
P113|A note to be printed along with the Section 60 certificate.
P114|Else, the SR clicks on the "Refuse Registration" button, officially rejecting the application. And the application follows the rejection flow.
