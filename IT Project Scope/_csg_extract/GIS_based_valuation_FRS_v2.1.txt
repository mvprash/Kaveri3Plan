P34|Introduction
P35|The Department of Stamps and Registration (henceforth referred to as “department”) is the custodian of registered public records such as Registration of Marriages and Firms, Documents about transactions namely sale, transfer or gift of immovable properties, Mortgage, Power of Attorneys, wills and many more. Maintenance and preservation of these critical public records is the prime responsibility of the department. The records or the information associated with registered records are to be made available to the public when requested through defined procedures. The department is also responsible for collecting stamp duty on behalf of the Government for specified transactions. The services are provided through 260 Sub-Registrar Offices (SROs), under the supervision of 35 District Registrar Offices, spread across the state.
P36|The department has computerized the delivery of these services through the development of the Kaveri software. KAVERI Online service is a web-based application of the department that provides an interface to the Applicants required for documents, marriage, and firm registration. It enables Applicants to know the details of stamp duty, property guidelines value, data entry for document registration and marriage registration, etc. The list of modules of Kaveri 2.0 is available in Annexure 1.
P37|Document scope
P38|This document is a Functional Requirement Specification (FRS) describing the functional features of the GIS-based market valuation. The document is prepared by the Centre for Smart Governance (CSG) and has been submitted for department approval. The intended audience of the document is the departmental approving authority and the development team. The document was prepared based on consultation with the Department of Stamps and Registrations. Based on the subsequent discussions, the FRS has been revised to incorporate the fundamental change in valuation policy from a manual road-based approach to an automated base rate approach.
P40|The document describes the high-level integration protocol between the Kaveri development team, Bhoomi team and the KSRSAC team. However, its scope excludes the algorithms to be adopted by the KSRSAC team. Further, this feature will be for agricultural properties.
P42|The current valuation module covers Agricultural land requirements as agreed upon with the department. There will not be any change in the market valuation logic of the Non-Agricultural properties.
P43|Description of the functional requirement
P44|Currently, stamp duty calculations are based on the “proper market value” of a property as determined by the Central Valuation Committee (CVC). However, rate fixing is prone to significant discretion and might potentially lead to a loss of revenue to the state treasury. The feature seeks to reduce users' discretion and maximise revenue realisation by automating market value determination to the extent feasible.
P45|Thus, there are only key changes to the calculation in the market valuation determination of agricultural properties.

P49|The system then shows the applicable Guideline Value as per the CVC guideline values.

P53|The Sub-Registrar currently has the discretion to add, delete, or modify the valuation attributes manually.
P54|These changes are performed without system-supported data validation.

P126|Market Valuation:
P127|The system automatically fetches the base rate of the village; this guideline value is based on inputs provided by the Bhoomi and KSRSAC using the logic below.
P128|The system will compare the agricultural rate against a certain percentage of the non-agricultural rate and will pick the higher rate. The percentage will be defined by the attributes provided by the KSRSAC.
P129|The system calculates the Market value using the formula:
P130|Market value = Base rate * transacted extent.
P131|There should be an option for the applicant to update (to increase only) the tree count and Built-up area.
P132|System calculates the total market value based on the user's input using the formula: Total Market value = Market Value + (tree count * rate/tree) + (Built up area*rate/sq. mtr)+ Other attribute value.
