Accounts  
 
i. Brief Description:  
The revenue generated are remitted to appropriate head of accounts and then reconciled with bank 
Challan and treasury report.  
Accounting is an internal function of the department. SRO does daily accountin g, remits the money 
to banks and records the remittances. The statements are prepared by SRO and sent to DRO. DRO 
further consolidates it and sends to IGR office. Reconciliation with treasuries is carried out at 
concerned SR/DR offices.  
This module will be  used for internal purpose by IGR/DR/SR.  
Types:  
 Add Funds  
 Allocate Funds  
 Expenditure  
 Reconciliation
