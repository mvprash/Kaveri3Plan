P50|Introduction
P52|The Department of Stamps and Registration (hereafter referred to as “department”) is the custodian of registered public records such as Registration of Marriages and Firms, Documents about transactions namely sale, transfer or gift of immovable properties, Mortgage, Power of Attorneys, wills and many more. Maintenance and preservation of these critical public records is the department's prime responsibility. The records or the information associated with registered records are to be made available to the public when requested through defined procedures. The department is also responsible for collecting stamp duty on behalf of the Government for specified transactions. The services are provided through 256 Sub-Registrar Offices (SROs), under the supervision of 34 District Registrar Offices, spread across the state.
P53|The department has computerised the delivery of these services through the development of Kaveri software. KAVERI Online service is a web-based application of the department which provides an interface to the citizens required for documents, marriage and firm registration. It enables citizens to know the details of stamp duty, property guidelines value, data entry for document registration and marriage registration etc. The list of modules of Kaveri 2.0 is available in Annexure 1.
P55|Document scope
P56|This document is a Functional Requirement Specification (FRS) describing the functional feature of handling documents kept pending in Kaveri 1 and the applicant wants to continue the registration of such documents in Kaveri 2.0 using the Pending document registration module. The document is prepared by the Centre for Smart Governance (CSG). The intended audience of the document is the departmental approving authority and the development team.
P57|The document was prepared through document/data analysis and departmental consultation. The high-level requirements were shared by the department (Annexure 2).
P60|Overview of the function
P61|The Pending Document Registration module is an integral component of the Document Registration system, enabling the registration of documents previously marked as pending in the Kaveri 1 system and transferred to the minute book. In the current workflow, citizens can resume the registration process in Kaveri 2 for a document that is pending in Kaveri 1 by entering the associated P-number and submitting the application via the Pending Document Registration module.
P63|Using this module, the SR can release such documents for registration in Kaveri 2, provided the citizen submits a DR order (if required). However, if the SR encounters issues retrieving specific details from Kaveri 1 such as document details, stamp information, receipts, index village, boundary, valuation, schedules, property information, or party details— the SR can update the missing details and the application will be forwarded to the DR for approval.
P65|Upon receiving the DR’s approval, SR can release the document from the minute book, allowing the registration process to proceed in Kaveri 2.

P152|Assumptions
P153|Applications with missing data will be added to the Minute Book only after all missing details are entered by the SR and approved by DR. Until then, such applications will remain under the Minute Book-others tab.
P154|The application rejected by the DR will be pushed back to the Minute Book Others tab.
P155|No fee will be charged to the applicant for submitting the application to the SR for editing the missing fields.
P156|The SR will not have the option to withdraw an application after the DR rejects the edited submission.
P157|K1 pending applications will follow the typical K2 pending applications flow after releasing from the Minute book.
P158|J-slip should be sent for the transaction.
P159|Limitations
P160|K1 pending applications follow the same process as K2 pending applications; therefore, no appointment booking is provided.
P161|Handling multiple pending reasons apart from the party's late appearance not is possible.
