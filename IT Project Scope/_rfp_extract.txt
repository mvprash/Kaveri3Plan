The various components that shall be exposed to the citizen over the internet/BangaloreOne, Karnataka One/Nemmadi are as follows:
a. Data Entry - This shall facilitate online basic data entry for the registration of documents, firms and marriages
b. Scheduling of Appointments - This shall facilitate scheduling of appointments for registration of documents, firms, societies and marriages
c. Encumbrance Certificate - This shall facilitate online application for Encumbrance Certificate and Online Encumbrance Search
d. Certified Copies - This shall facilitate online application for certified copies.
e. Market Valuation and Stamp Duty (SD) & Registration Fee (RF) estimation - This module shall facilitate calculation of Estimated Market Value for the property and also estimate the Stamp Duty and Registration Fee for registration of documents.
f. Tracking of application status can also be done using the internet.

1. The applicant will be able to register on the portal with login/password and some basic information including e-mail etc, or use a guest login to initiate any form of application.
2. The applicant will be allowed to save incomplete applications and access them again through the portal using their login and password and complete the application within a time period.
3. The applicant logs on to the service delivery portal of the department. He selects the service "Document Registration" from the list of available services. He selects from the list, the SRO in whose jurisdiction the property belongs as well as the SRO where he wants to register the document. The online e-Form for entering the data is displayed. The instruction to fill the application online is available on the page.
4. The online form will have separate tabs for entering different information type such as Personal detail of parties and witnesses, document details, property details and schedules. The applicant fills up the form and submits online.
5. The system will validate the application. If the application is invalid or incomplete, or the land is barred from registration as per data received from Bhoomi (Govt land, granted land, etc) system will reject application (before initiating process for booking appointment) and notify the reason through a pop-up message giving the option to the applicant to modify application or exit. If the applicant wants to modify the application, he is redirected to the online form for resubmission. Else he will save/print the "notification for rejection" and exit. 
6. In case the property to be registered is agricultural the system will retrieve the details of seller name (present owner), extent, etc from Bhoomi database in the backend and display it to the applicant for confirmation. After confirmation the details will be auto populated in the e-forms. 
7. The applicant can check available time slots in the relevant SROs on the portal and opt for suitable date, time and SRO available for appointment. 
8. The applicant will then have to pay the required Appointment Fee through the payment gateway provided in the portal for confirmation of appointment.
9. Upon confirmation the system displays the details of the Appointment and a unique appointment code for printing. The appointment details will also be sent through sms/e-mail.
10. The citizen can obtain Estimated Market Value, stamp duty and registration fee online before or while filing an application. 
11. The SR will log on to the system in the SRO. He will find the list of online applications logged to his screen scheduled for the particular day. The parties will visit the SR on specified time and date. They will enter the application number/appointment code in the Kiosk at SRO and mark their presence for presentation of document. The SR will call the parties to present the document on scheduled time. The parties will present to the SR with the documents and required enclosures.
12. The SR using a checklist prompted by the system will do the following checks:
o Date of execution and presentation of the deed
o Admission by parties for voluntary execution of the deed
o Classification of the document
o Property locations and schedules mentioned in the deed
o Check for any Court Orders related to the said property as indicated by the Court Order Module.
o Verify Estimated Market Value and stamp duty for the given property details (which must be same as mentioned  in deed)
o Validate payment in case of e-Stamping from the SHCIL server
o Validation of the testimonials - RTC, Khata, PAN, Revenue sketch, affidavit and Form 1
o Verify Data entered in the system vis-à-vis the physical documents and testimonials
o Check for impersonation by matching of thumb records/photograph (current seller with previous buyer in index II record).
o The system should also be able to integrate with Aadhar system (developed by Unique Identification Authority of India - UIDAI) and interface with it to utilize authentication services/APIs provided by the Aadhar system wherein the biometric/thumb impression captured in the New Kaveri system is sent securely to the Aadhar system and the authentication information is received back for verification. (The details of the requirements for the New Kaveri application to integrate with the Aadhar/UID system are provided in Annexure 4 of this document).
o Checks for duplicate and multiple registration done through index II validation online for the same schedule of property
o Check if the property is banned from registration by law or court order by checking the information provided by the Court Order Entry Module.
o The SR will also validate the data furnished in RTC, survey sketches, Khata and PAN through online access of databases of Bhoomi, SSLR, concerned ULB and IT department. (However, this integration is subject to readiness of the databases of concerned departments. The system should have the feature to validate the document with these external databases whenever they are made available.)
13. On complete validation, the SR will admit the document for registration and ask the parties to pay the stamp duty and registration fees. The payment details are entered in A-Register in the system by SR and the generated receipt is given to the presenters. The buyer signs the Form 60/61 and Form 81-A as applicable and submits it to SRO. SR asks the FDA/SDA/operator to capture photograph and thumb impression of the parties.
14. The SR will not accept/admit the document for registration in following scenarios:
In case:
a. The document is refused for registration as the SR finds it barred from registration after his Scrutiny. An entry is made in book 2 as reason for refusal. He endorses the word "Registration Refused" on the document. On application made by executants or claimants, SR gives him a copy of the reason so recorded without any payment. The entry is also made in the minute book. In case of refusal to accept the document, the system shall generate the refusal number and mandatorily record the reason for refusal in the system. The citizen can avail the information service to obtain Estimated Market Value, stamp duty and registration fee online.
Online:

3. Citizen selects the location details like district, taluka, village and street as applicable. 
4. Part of the screen displays the corresponding survey maps from SSLR records showing the demarcated survey numbers, streets and key landmarks.
5. The citizen can either select the survey number by moving the cursor over the property, demarcated in the survey map, he wants to value or he enters the corresponding survey number in the text box in the form.
6. The form will also display the list of survey numbers, along with the Right-holder's detail, available in Bhoomi Database for the specified location details mentioned above. 
7. The citizen can see the boundaries and extent of the properties in displayed survey maps on the screen. With the help of the map and list of survey numbers, the citizen is able to identify the property in case he is not having the Survey number with him.

11. Based on the entered data, the system calculates the EMV of the property and generates the guidance slip.

13. The system will return the stamp duty/registration fee payable.

The described property is selected on the map and the complete schedule as found in Bhoomi database with extent, boundaries and other relevant information for that property is populated on the screen. 
3. In case, the citizen furnishes the Survey number, the SR or his FDA/SDA will simply enter survey number in the Form and retrieve relevant data from Bhoomi/UPR database as the case may be.
4. The instruction to fill the application online is available on the page. 2. The applicant fills up the form and submits online. The required fields to be filled are same as in physical form 22 such as Name and address of the applicant, Names and addresses of the Executants and Claimants, Document registration number, year of registration and the corresponding Book Type, Volume no., Page and date. The email address and mobile number of the applicant will also be recorded for notification of the status, if opted for. The citizen will specify the mode of delivery of certified copy (Post/In Person).
3. The system will validate the application. If the application is invalid or incomplete, it will reject application and notify the reason through a pop-up message giving the option to the applicant to modify application or exit. If the applicant wants to modify the application, he is redirected to the online form for resubmission. Else he will save/print the "notification for rejection", endorsement and exit. In case of successful application, the system will calculate fees based on number of pages in the document (retrieved by system) and open the available payment gateways. The applicant will select the suitable payment option, enter the payment details and pay the fees online.
4. If payment is not successful, the transaction and application is rejected and the reason is notified through a pop-up message giving the option to the applicant to retry the payment option through payment gateway or exit. If the payment is successful, the application number is generated along with a receipt in printable form. The applicant can save or print the receipt for future reference. The application is now logged to the SR in the system in order of entries. The entry of transaction is made in A Register automatically.
