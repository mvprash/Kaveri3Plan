NOTIFICATION 
In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 78 of the Registration Act, 1908 
(Central Act 16 of 1908), the Government of Kamataka hereby substitutes the 
Registration fee as below, prescribed in Article I( 4 )(a), III(a)(i) and III(a)(ii) of Table 
of Registration Fees, notified vide Notification No. RD 403 ESR 85, Dated 27th May, 
1986 and amendment notifications issued from time to time. 
1) In Article 1(4)(a), "For every Rs. 1,000 or part thereof-10-00", "For every Rs. 
1,000 or part thereof-20-00", shall be substituted. 
2) In Article III(a)(i), in the entries relating thereto, for the word "one rupee", the 
word "two rupee" shall be substituted. 
3) In Article Ill(a)(ii), in the entries relating thereto, for the word "one rupee", the 
word "two rupee" shall be substituted. 
This notification shall come into effect from 31.08.2025
